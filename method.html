The Method

Seven phases. One system.

01

Diagnose

Business, brand, audience, positioning, existing content and competitive environment, examined directly. You can't fix what you haven't properly understood.

02

Position

The territory the brand should own, and what makes it different, made explicit. What should people associate with you? What ideas can you own?

03

Structure

Positioning converted into pillars, themes, formats, messaging and workflow. This is where strategy becomes usable and repeatable.

04

Create

Scripts, video, carousels and campaigns, each with a defined reason for existing. Everything has a purpose behind it.

05

Distribute

The right content, on the right platform, in the right format, for the right audience. Distribution is as important as creation.

06

Measure

Performance, audience response and content quality, reviewed on evidence. What is actually working, and what needs to change?

07

Compound

The system improved based on what the evidence shows — not reinvented from scratch. Good content systems get better over time.

Strategy is not a document. It is a decision-making system.
